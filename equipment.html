Available equipment

Equipment we arrange

Tell us what the doctor has advised for the patient. We'll confirm availability and charges before anything is delivered.

Hospital bed

Adjustable bed with side rails for bedridden or recovering patients. It makes turning, feeding and daily care easier and safer.

Oxygen concentrator

Gives a continuous supply of oxygen at home for patients on oxygen therapy, at the flow rate their doctor prescribes.

BiPAP

Breathing support machine for patients with breathing difficulties, used with the settings advised by their doctor.

Pulse oximeter

Small clip-on device that checks blood oxygen level and pulse in seconds, so families can keep an eye on readings.

Oxygen cylinder

Portable oxygen supply for backup during power cuts, for travel, or for patients who need oxygen for short periods.

Suction machine

Clears mucus and secretions from the airway for patients who cannot clear it themselves.

How it works

From request to set up at home

  1. Tell us

    Message us on WhatsApp or book online with the equipment the patient needs.

  2. We confirm

    Our team confirms availability, delivery time and charges with you.

  3. Delivered & set up

    The equipment is delivered to the patient's home and set up ready to use.

  4. Care support

    Our nurses can help the family use the equipment as part of the patient's care.
