Our services

Care for every stage of recovery

Tell us what the patient needs when you book. If you're unsure, describe the situation and our team will advise.

Qualified Nurses

Trained male and female nurses for injections, drips, dressings and daily nursing care at home.

Patient Attendants / Caregivers

Attendants who help with feeding, mobility, toileting and keeping the patient comfortable.

Elderly & Senior Care

Gentle day-to-day care and company for elderly and bedridden family members.

Post-Hospital Care

Recovery support after surgery or discharge, following the doctor's care plan.

Medication Support

Medicines given on time, as prescribed, with a record kept for the family and doctor.

Physiotherapy at Home

Exercises and therapy at home to rebuild strength, balance and movement.

Personal Care & Hygiene

Bathing, grooming, changing and bed care done with dignity and respect.

Medical Equipment Assistance

Hospital beds, oxygen and monitoring equipment arranged and set up at home.

12 / 24-Hour Care

Day shifts, night shifts or round-the-clock care, depending on the patient's needs.

How it works

  1. Book

    Fill in the appointment form or message us on WhatsApp.

  2. We call you

    Our team confirms the patient's needs, timing and charges.

  3. Staff arrives

    A trained nurse or caregiver reaches the patient's home.

  4. Ongoing care

    Regular visits or shifts, with updates for the family.
